Places in this repository
=========================

Source: Overture Maps Foundation, Places theme
        https://overturemaps.org
        https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/

The extract in `data/places-jabodetabek.parquet` was changed from the
published theme:

  - only records whose point sits inside the Jabodetabek box
    (106.4, -6.7 to 107.2, -6.05) are kept;
  - records below the Overture confidence of 0.5 are dropped;
  - permanently and temporarily closed records are dropped;
  - records with the same name within about eleven metres are reduced
    to the one with the highest confidence;
  - only the identifier, name, category, address, locality, confidence
    and coordinate are kept.

The release used by the snapshot is written in
`data/places-jabodetabek.json`.
