Pedestrian bridges (JPO) and footbridges in Daily Route
=======================================================

The registry in data/jpo-network.json is generated by `pnpm data:jpo`.

Official points
---------------
Source: Dinas Bina Marga Provinsi DKI Jakarta, "JPO Bina Marga 2024",
published through Jakarta Satu.
Service: https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/Dinas_Bina_Marga/JPO_Bina_Marga_2024/FeatureServer
Retrieved: 2026-11-05.
The layer carries 203 points, PERIODE_DA 2025. The data is the city's
asset record: a point per bridge, with its address, type and remark. It does
not carry the bridge line, stairs, lifts, opening hours or an entrance side.

OpenStreetMap
-------------
Source: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
Query: highway=footway or highway=pedestrian with bridge=* or tunnel=* in
the Jabodetabek box (south -6.7, west 106.4, north -6.05, east 107.2).
Retrieved: 2026-11-05.
The geometry is the line the bridge follows. An unnamed park bridge is kept
only when it stands beside a transit stop; a named JPO or skywalk is kept
even when it is not beside a stop. The registry is therefore transit access,
not every footbridge in Jakarta.

Matching
--------
An official point is matched to the nearest OSM bridge line within 80 m.
192 of the 203 official points matched; 11 remain point-only and are drawn
as points, not as routable lines. OSM bridges already used by an official
point are not repeated. A stop within 120 m of an end is linked to the
bridge.

Limits
------
The official geometry exists only for DKI Jakarta. Bodetabek is represented
by OpenStreetMap only and is marked `official: false`. An underpass is not a
JPO unless the source says so; the official JPO layer does not include the
Kuningan pedestrian underpass. The registry does not invent a name for an
unnamed OSM bridge, and it does not claim stairs or a lift that the source
does not tag.
